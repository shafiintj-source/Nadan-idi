ENGLISH TITLE
Naadan Adi: Kerala 2D Shooter

ENGLISH SHORT DESCRIPTION
Jetpack battles with a Kerala vibe. Practise solo or play in rooms with friends.

ENGLISH FULL DESCRIPTION
Bring your friends to a Kerala-inspired 2D arena.

Naadan Adi combines jetpack movement, quick rounds and Malayalam flavour. Fly between platforms, choose your weapon and find your rhythm in a colourful monsoon setting.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Practise against bots before inviting your friends.
• Create an online private room and share its six-character code.
• Play with 2–6 players in an online room.
• Use the LAN host and join options on a shared Wi-Fi network.
• Enjoy three-minute rounds with SMG, shotgun and rifle loadouts.
• Choose from six outfit colour variations.
• Read Malayalam reactions during the action.

KERALA FLAVOUR
Mundu-inspired outfits, tea-shop scenery and Malayalam touches give the arena a familiar local character. The characters are original fictional designs.

FRIENDS, READY, START
Create a room, share the code and wait for everyone to tap Ready. The host starts the match. Internet is required for online rooms. Local network play requires compatible devices on a network that permits peer connections.

This version has no ads or in-app purchases. Device performance and network conditions can affect gameplay.
